# Business Impact Analysis: DHCP Implementation

## ROI Analysis (50-employee company)

| Cost Factor | Without DHCP | With DHCP | Annual Savings |
|-------------|--------------|-----------|----------------|
| IT Labor (onboarding) | $5,000 | $500 | $4,500 |
| IP Conflict Resolution | $3,000 | $200 | $2,800 |
| Employee Downtime | $8,000 | $1,000 | $7,000 |
| Documentation & Auditing | $2,000 | $500 | $1,500 |
| **Total** | **$18,000** | **$2,200** | **$15,800** |

## Business Use Cases

### Small Business (10-50 employees)
- **Challenge**: No dedicated IT staff
- **Solution**: Single router with DHCP manages all IP assignments
- **Benefit**: $5,000+ annual IT cost savings

### Enterprise Environment
- Multiple DHCP servers for redundancy
- Scope segregation by department (HR, Finance, IT)
- Reservation for critical devices (printers, servers)

## Scalability Analysis

| Company Size | DHCP Servers | Scopes | Management Complexity |
|--------------|--------------|--------|----------------------|
| Small (50 devices) | 1 | 1-2 | Low |
| Medium (200 devices) | 2 | 5-10 | Medium |
| Large (1000+ devices) | 4+ | 20+ | High |

## Risk Mitigation Value

| Business Risk | Without DHCP | With DHCP | Risk Reduction |
|---------------|--------------|-----------|----------------|
| IP Address Conflicts | High | None | 100% |
| Network Downtime | 10+ hours/year | 2 hours/year | 80% |
| Security Incidents | Moderate | Low | 60% |
